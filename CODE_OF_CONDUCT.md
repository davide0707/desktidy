# Code of conduct

DeskTidy is a small, friendly project. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere the
project is represented) is expected to help keep it that way.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to programming.
- Assume good intentions; ask before you judge.
- Give and take criticism of ideas and code, never of people.
- Respect other people's privacy: never post someone's files, paths, names or personal data.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or demeaning remarks of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, threats, and personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any other behaviour that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [@davide0707](https://github.com/davide0707), using the contact on
the maintainer's GitHub profile, or with GitHub's *Report content*. Reports are read promptly and kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, and warn or ban contributors, when this code of conduct is not
respected. Security vulnerabilities are not conduct issues: report them as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
